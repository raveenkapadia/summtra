How to Read This Report

1 Planetary Lines
Colored lines on maps represent planetary energies. Thicker, glowing lines are most relevant to your selected goal. Each planet has a unique influence on different aspects of life.
2 City Markers
Gold dots indicate recommended cities, sized by their score. Red dots mark cities to avoid. Your birthplace is shown with a special star marker for reference.
3 Power Zones
Glowing circular areas show where beneficial planetary lines cross. These zones amplify positive energies. Green = beneficial, Yellow = mixed, Red = challenging.
4 Match Scores
Each city receives a percentage score (0-100%) based on planetary line proximity, Vedic compatibility, Vastu alignment, and current Dasha timing.
