What is Astrocartography?

Astrocartography, also known as locational astrology, is a powerful technique that maps your birth chart onto the Earth, revealing how different locations around the world resonate with your unique cosmic blueprint.

Developed by astrologer Jim Lewis in the 1970s, this system shows where planetary energies are most strongly expressed for you personally. By understanding these influences, you can make informed decisions about where to live, work, travel, or pursue specific life goals.

☉
Planetary Lines
Lines crossing the map show where each planet was rising, setting, or at its peak during your birth moment. These create corridors of energy unique to you.
⊕
Power Zones
Where beneficial planetary lines cross, powerful zones of opportunity emerge. These intersections amplify positive energies for specific life goals.
☽
Vedic Integration
We enhance Western astrocartography with Vedic astrology—your Rashi, Nakshatra, and Lagna—plus Vastu directional principles for deeper insight.
⏱
Timing Matters
Your Vimshottari Dasha periods reveal when certain locations become more favorable. The right place at the right time multiplies success.
