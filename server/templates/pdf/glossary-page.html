Glossary of Terms

Astrocartography
A technique mapping planetary lines across Earth based on your birth chart, showing where planetary energies are strongest.
Lagna
लग्न
Your Ascendant or rising sign at birth — the zodiac sign on the eastern horizon at your exact birth moment.
Rashi
राशि
Your Moon sign in Vedic astrology — the zodiac sign where the Moon was positioned at birth.
Nakshatra
नक्षत्र
One of 27 lunar mansions in Vedic astrology. Your birth nakshatra reveals deeper personality traits.
Mahadasha
महादशा
A major planetary period lasting years. Each planet rules for a specific duration in sequence.
Antardasha
अंतर्दशा
A sub-period within the Mahadasha, lasting months, adding nuance to the major period's themes.
Paran Line
Where two planetary lines cross at the same latitude, creating a zone of combined planetary influence.
Orb
The distance from a planetary line. Closer orb = stronger influence. Measured in degrees or kilometers.
