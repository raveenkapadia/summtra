Terms & Definitions

Glossary

Astrocartography Terms
Astrocartography
A technique that maps your birth chart onto the Earth, showing where planetary energies are strongest for you.
Planetary Line
A line on the map where a specific planet was at a particular angle (rising, setting, culminating) at your birth moment.
Ascendant (AC)
The eastern horizon point. AC lines show where a planet affects your personality, physical self, and first impressions.
Descendant (DC)
The western horizon point. DC lines influence relationships, partnerships, and how you connect with others.
Midheaven (MC)
The highest point in the sky. MC lines affect career, public reputation, and life direction.
Imum Coeli (IC)
The lowest point in the sky. IC lines influence home, family, roots, and emotional foundation.
Power Zone
An area where two or more planetary lines cross or run close together, amplifying their combined energies.
Paran Line
A horizontal latitude line where two planets share the same angular relationship to the horizon.
Vedic Astrology Terms
Rashi (राशि)
Your Moon sign in Vedic astrology, representing your emotional nature and inner self.
Nakshatra (नक्षत्र)
One of 27 lunar mansions. Your birth star reveals deeper personality traits and favorable directions.
Lagna (लग्न)
Your Vedic ascendant or rising sign, determining your physical constitution and life approach.
Dasha (दशा)
Planetary period system showing which planet's energy dominates during specific life phases.
Mahadasha
Major planetary period lasting several years, setting the overall theme for that life phase.
Antardasha
Sub-period within a Mahadasha, refining the planetary influence for shorter durations.
Vastu & Direction Terms
Vastu (वास्तु)
Ancient Indian science of directional harmony, indicating favorable directions based on your Lagna.
Favorable Direction
A compass direction that supports your goals based on Vastu and Nakshatra calculations.
