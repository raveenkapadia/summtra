Glossary of Terms

AC Line (Ascendant)
Where a planet was rising on the eastern horizon. Impacts personal identity and how others perceive you.
DC Line (Descendant)
Where a planet was setting on the western horizon. Influences relationships and partnerships.
MC Line (Midheaven)
Where a planet was at its highest point. Affects career, public image, and reputation.
IC Line (Imum Coeli)
Where a planet was at its lowest point. Relates to home, family, and inner foundations.
Yogakaraka
योगकारक
A planet ruling both a Kendra (angular) and Trikona (trine) house, bringing exceptional benefits.
Vimshottari Dasha
विंशोत्तरी दशा
The 120-year planetary cycle system used in Vedic astrology to time life events.
