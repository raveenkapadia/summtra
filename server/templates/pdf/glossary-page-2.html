Planetary Influences
Sun (सूर्य)
Represents self-expression, vitality, authority, and recognition. Strong near MC lines for career prominence.
Moon (चंद्र)
Governs emotions, comfort, and nurturing. Strongest near IC lines for feeling at home.
Mercury (बुध)
Rules communication, learning, and commerce. Beneficial for education and business dealings.
Venus (शुक्र)
Planet of love, beauty, and pleasure. Enhances relationships and creative pursuits.
Mars (मंगल)
Energy, drive, and assertion. Can bring success through action but may cause conflicts.
Jupiter (गुरु)
The great benefic. Brings expansion, luck, wisdom, and prosperity wherever its lines fall.
Saturn (शनि)
Discipline, structure, and lessons. Can bring long-term success through hard work and patience.
Rahu (राहु)
North lunar node. Represents ambition, worldly desires, and unconventional paths to success.
